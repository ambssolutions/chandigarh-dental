Questions

Before you come in.

Where is the clinic?

On Morinda Road in Chamkaur Sahib, opposite the bus stand and near Supreme Agencies. Postcode 140112.

When are you open?

Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 7 PM. The clinic is closed on Sundays.

Which treatments do you offer?

Root canal treatment, laser dentistry, teeth straightening, tooth reshaping, oral and maxillofacial surgery, and maxillofacial prosthetics. Call to ask about anything not listed.

How do I book?

Call +91 99155 10261, or fill in the form on this page. It opens WhatsApp with your details ready to send.

Do I need to book an appointment in advance?

Booking ahead by phone or WhatsApp is the easiest way to get a time that suits you. If it is urgent, say so when you call.

Can you help with a sudden toothache?

Call or WhatsApp as soon as you can and tell us you are in pain. The dentist can examine the tooth, and root canal treatment and extractions are among the treatments offered.

Do you do braces, fillings or implants?

Teeth straightening, dental filling and dental implant services are all listed for the clinic on Justdial, along with root canal, laser dentistry and oral surgery. Call to confirm the treatment you need.

Do you offer teeth whitening?

Whitening is not one of the treatments listed for this clinic. Call and ask before you visit.

Do you treat children?

The clinic is listed under children's dentistry (paedodontist) on Justdial. Call to ask about your child's age and treatment.
