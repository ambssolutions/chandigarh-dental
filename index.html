Treatments

No second trip to Ropar or Chandigarh for a specialist.

  1. 01

    Root canal (RCT)

    Clears the infection inside the tooth, seals it, and lets you keep the tooth you have.

  2. 02

    Laser dentistry

    Precise soft-tissue treatment with less bleeding and faster healing.

  3. 03

    Braces / straightening

    Braces and alignment for crowding, gaps and a bite that does not close.

  4. 04

    Tooth reshaping

    Smooths and rebuilds chipped or uneven teeth so they look natural.

  5. 05

    Dental fillings

    Repairs a cavity early, before it reaches the nerve.

  6. 06

    Dental implants

    A fixed replacement for a missing tooth, set in the jaw.

  7. 07

    Teeth whitening

    Lightens stains from tea, food and tobacco, under the dentist's care.

  8. 08

    Oral & maxillofacial surgery

    Extractions, wisdom teeth, surgical care of the mouth and jaw, and maxillofacial prosthetics.
